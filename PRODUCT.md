# Product: Signature Home Renovations LLC — pitch one-pager

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system fonts
- Primary device: phone, opened from a Facebook link inside Facebook's in-app browser

## Users
1. **The owner** of Signature Home Renovations LLC (name unknown). Sees this as a free sample site in an outreach message. Must recognise their own business, done better than their flyer, in the first phone screen.
2. **Homeowners in South Jersey** who saw the ad in a Facebook contractor/community group and want a free estimate for a bathroom, deck, floor, paint job or repair.

Give a lead-hunting contractor (posting the same ad in 4 NJ Facebook groups on 2026-10-08, with no website, Maps listing or reviews) one link that sells the work and turns a visit into a text or call.
